Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and route your data when you purchase our products.

Data Collection & Merchant of Record

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns. Your payment data is collected securely by Paddle; we do not store or process your credit card information on our servers.

Third-Party Data Routing (Fulfillment Architecture)

To fulfill your purchase, your email address is securely transmitted from Paddle via webhook to Make.com. Make.com acts as our automated integration layer, which then authenticates with the GitHub REST API to securely invite your email address as an outside collaborator to our private scaffolding repository. By making a purchase, you consent to this specific data routing solely for the purpose of product fulfillment.
