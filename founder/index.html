About the founder

Kai Silva

Founder, Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC. 10+ years in solutions engineering and data. Builds the platform and the product on top of it.

Kai has spent 10+ years in solutions engineering and data, from start-ups and consulting (West Monroe, CompassX Group) to enterprise data platforms. Today that means Databricks lakehouses on AWS, dbt transformation layers, and the product decisions about what a data team should build next. Across every platform, the same plumbing gets rebuilt by hand: state backends, Medallion layers, CI gates, environment promotion.

He started Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC in 2026 to package that repeated work into products other teams can buy once and own. Kairos Data Systems came first. Rackside came from his own garage gym, where he wanted training that knew what equipment was actually on the floor.

Both products are designed, built and supported by Kai directly. If you write to info@kairoscc.io, he reads it.
