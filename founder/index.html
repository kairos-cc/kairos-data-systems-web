About the founder

Malachi “Kai” Silva

Founder, Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC. Data engineer and data product owner who builds the platform and the product on top of it.

Kai works on enterprise data platforms: Databricks lakehouses on AWS, dbt transformation layers, and the product decisions about what a data team should build next. Most of that work repeats from project to project. The same state backends, the same Medallion layers, the same CI gates, rebuilt by hand each time.

He started Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC in 2026 to package that repeated work into products other teams can buy once and own. Kairos Data Systems came first. Rackside came from his own garage gym, where he wanted training that knew what equipment was actually on the floor.

Both products are designed, built and supported by Kai directly. If you write to info@kairoscc.io, he reads it.
